Scan or Photograph Your Pages

🖨️ Scan at College

  • Page flat on the scanner glass
  • Swipe card → Scan to Email
  • Resolution: 300 dpi
  • Download from your inbox

📸 Photograph

  • Soft daylight — no direct sun
  • Phone directly above, not at an angle
  • Take several, pick the sharpest
  • Check it's in focus before moving on
Best results: scan for clarity, photograph for texture.
